CENTRAL MINE PLANNING AND DESIGN INSTITUTE LIMITED (CMPDI)
REGIONAL INSTITUTE - I, ASANSOL
GEOLOGICAL EXPLORATION & DETAILED DRILLING SUMMARY REPORT

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
During FY 2023-24, CMPDI undertook exploratory core drilling in Mand-Raigarh Coalfield.
ECL recorded raw coal production of 35.40 MT across all open cast mines.
SECL achieved raw coal production of 167.0 MT during the reporting period.

2. EXPLORATION AND DRILLING METRICS
Total core drilling achieved was 12450 m across 42 boreholes.
In Mand-Raigarh Block IV, geological reserve is estimated at 485.50 MT.
Proved reserve identified stands at 312.80 MT in Seam III and Seam IV.
Average stripping ratio calculated for the opencast block is 3.45 m3/t.

3. SUBSIDIARY PERFORMANCE
BCCL reported overburden removal of 48.50 Mm3 in FY 2023-24.
Coal stock at pithead was 8.40 MT at the close of Q4.
Exploration area covered during the survey was 28.50 sq km.
All geological data verified in accordance with Indian Standard Procedure (ISP-2017).
