GEOLOGICAL EXPLORATION & DRILLING SUMMARY REPORT
BLOCK: BOKARO WEST COALFIELD SECTOR IV
REPORTING PERIOD: FY 2024-25 (QUARTER 2)

1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
CMPDI RI-II carried out detailed exploration in the Western Bokaro coalfield sector covering an area of 14.2 sq. km.
A total of 42 exploratory boreholes were drilled during the reporting quarter using hydrostatic core drill rigs.

2. DRILLING PERFORMANCE SUMMARY
- Total Drilling Target for Q2: 12,500 meters
- Actual Drilling Achieved: 13,120 meters (Achievement: 104.9%)
- Average Core Recovery Rate: 91.4%
- CBM / Gas Content Desorption tests performed: 18 samples

3. SEAM STRATIGRAPHY & RESERVE ESTIMATION
Seam X (Top): Thickness 4.8 meters to 6.2 meters, Non-coking Coal Grade G7.
Seam IX: Thickness 2.1 meters, Semi-coking Coal Grade W-IV.
Proved Geological Coal Reserves established: 48.60 Million Tonnes.
Indicated Coal Reserves: 24.30 Million Tonnes.
Inferred Reserves: 12.10 Million Tonnes.

4. HYDROGEOLOGICAL & ENVIRONMENTAL OBSERVATIONS
Depth of water table observed between 4.2m to 8.7m below ground level.
Heavy overburden strata composed primarily of coarse-grained sandstones and carbonaceous shales.
No major tectonic displacement or structural faulting detected across the primary strike zone.
